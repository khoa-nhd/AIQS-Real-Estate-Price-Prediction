## Access limitations

- **The Taste skill only accepts live URLs.** Its own error table says that for a file path it must ask whether a hosted version exists, and it never opens local files. Its capture step (browser screenshot plus a DOM extractor reading computed styles) is impossible for images and video.
- **No URL appears in any reference.** All five are cropped to the page, with no browser address bar.
- **At least two are design presentations, not live sites.** design_01 is a case-study layout and design_03 ends on a showcase caption. design_02, design_04 and design_05 may or may not exist as live pages; this inventory cannot tell.
- **Exact values are unavailable from images.** Fonts, spacing, radii and shadows can only be estimated from pixels. The recordings are compressed and downscaled, so small text and hex labels are only partly legible.
- **Third-party brands.** Puzzle, leap, Increase, Payal and Finotive are other companies' names and marks. A #REP variant may follow a reference's visual system but must not reuse its name, logo or copy.
